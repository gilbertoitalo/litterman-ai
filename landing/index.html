How it works

From spoken insight to optimized allocation in seconds.

01 — LISTEN 🎙️

Speak your view

Describe a macro event, earnings signal, or geopolitical development in plain English.

02 — EXTRACT 🧠

Gemini extracts views

Gemini 2.5 Flash classifies the news and extracts structured P/Q matrices for the BL model — grounded with live search.

03 — OPTIMIZE ⚙️

Black-Litterman runs

Market equilibrium priors are blended with your views via Bayesian optimization with turnover and concentration constraints.

04 — RESPOND 📊

Voice + dashboard

The agent speaks the recommendation aloud and the live dashboard updates in real time via Firestore.
